How To Play

Knock the ball into the opponent's goal. Ball hits harder the faster you're going. Grab boost pads to fill your tank. Beat the Hard AI in Single Player to earn a drop — open it for a random car paint, wheel color, boost color, or goal celebration (Common through Mythic), then equip your favorites in the Garage from the main menu.

Sign in, then hit Online Match to be paired with another signed-in player. You always drive with the Player 1 controls (WASD, Space, Shift) — whichever side of the field you're placed on.
